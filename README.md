# 🍽️ Mamba Insight AI

> Restaurant Intelligence Platform powered by Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Mamba Insight AI helps restaurant owners and managers improve food quality, consistency, customer satisfaction, and operational performance.

Instead of simply displaying reports, Mamba Insight analyzes restaurant data and provides AI-powered recommendations to improve daily operations.

---

# ✨ Features

## 📊 Operations Dashboard

- Live Restaurant Health Score
- Revenue Metrics
- Customer Ratings
- Prep Time Tracking
- Operational Alerts
- Interactive Charts

## 🤖 AI Restaurant Coach

AI analyzes restaurant performance and provides:

- Strengths
- Warnings

- Expected Business Impact
